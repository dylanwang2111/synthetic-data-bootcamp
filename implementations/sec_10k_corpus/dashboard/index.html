Phase 7

Validation: does the synthetic narrative hold up?

Every item file for every fictional company, checked against its own financials.json and spec.json scenario: every dollar figure and percentage traced back to source, no real company name or ticker leaking in, every required statement heading present, and — for each company with a scenario — the scenario actually grounded in the narrative text, not just recorded in the spec.

Phase 9

QA pairs and retrieval evaluation

Every question has an exact answer and a gold passage. Synthetic questions are generated deterministically from each company's own financial statements and scenario facts — no model call, so the answer is guaranteed to be a verbatim substring of its gold passage by construction. Real questions are hand-authored from a 6-company real-filing sample, each checked against the actual text before being kept. Three retrievers — BM25, a real semantic embedding retriever, and their reciprocal-rank-fusion hybrid — are scored on recall@5, recall@10, MRR and nDCG@10, across three corpus scopes.

Verdict

Synthetic is a reasonable proxy for which retriever to pick — the top choice agrees with real — and for exercising the retrieval pipeline end to end. It is not a proxy for realistic document length, numeric density, or absolute retrieval-score magnitude: all three differ by a large, now-quantified margin. Treat synthetic retrieval results as directional for retriever choice, not as an estimate of real-world recall or score values.
